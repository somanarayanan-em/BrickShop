# INC-002 — Phrase parser

## Executive Summary

This increment adds the in-app parser that turns a search phrase into price, theme, and age bounds. The search page does not change yet.

Requirements 0.3 need exact comparisons. The parser owns those comparisons. A model is not part of this increment.

Reversed between-pairs are ordered. A between-pair or a hyphen pair is price unless the phrase says age or years old. Amounts may be decimals. A blank phrase after trim yields no bounds.

## Increment Identity

#### Increment Identity

**Increment ID**: INC-002
**Increment Name**: Phrase parser
**FR Mapping**:

- FR1.1.1 When the user submits a phrase on the search page, the system shall read it for a price bound, a theme fragment, and an age bound, and shall drop every word that is none of those. A price bound is a lower limit, an upper limit, or both. An age bound is one age or an age range.
- FR1.1.6 A price limit shall use the English price before the discount, not the Vietnamese price and not the discounted amount. "Under 50" means list price `<= 50`. "Over 50" means list price `>= 50`. "Between 10 and 50" means list price `>= 10` and `<= 50`. Both ends count.

## Goal

#### Goal

The shop has a pure function that returns inclusive price bounds, an age bound, and a theme fragment from a phrase, or no bounds when the phrase has none of those.

## Dependencies

#### Dependencies

INC-001 Test runner

## Plan

#### Plan

1. Create `lib/phrase-limits.js` exporting `parsePhrase(phrase)`.
2. Trim the phrase. Return no bounds for an empty result.
3. Read `under N`, `over N`, and `between A and B` as inclusive list-price bounds. Accept decimals. When A is greater than B, set the lower bound to the smaller number.
4. Treat `between A and B` and a hyphen pair `A-B` as price unless the phrase says age or years old. Read `age N` and `N years old` as a single age. Read `ages A-B`, `A-B years old`, and `between A and B years old` as an age range.
5. Put unconsumed tokens into `theme`. Drop words that are not price, theme, or age.
6. Export `ageOverlaps(storedAge, ageMin, ageMax)` for stored `A-B` and `N+`, including endpoints. Return false for any other stored text.
7. Add the design's parser and age cases to `test/phrase-limits.test.js`, plus reversed between, a decimal amount, a blank stored age, and a spaces-only phrase.
8. Do not import `@huggingface/transformers`.

## Outcome

#### Outcome

- `parsePhrase` returns `priceMin`, `priceMax`, `ageMin`, `ageMax`, and `theme`, each absent when unused
- Absent `ageMax` with `ageMin` set means N+
- `ageOverlaps` implements the overlap rule
- `npm test` covers the cases in the design testing strategy and the planning defaults

## Automated Verification (AI Gate)

#### Automated Verification (AI Gate)

1. Run `npm test`.

**Expected**:

- `under 50` sets `priceMax` to 50
- `over 50` sets `priceMin` to 50
- `between 10 and 50` sets both bounds
- `between 50 and 10` sets `priceMin` 10 and `priceMax` 50
- `between 5 and 12` sets `priceMin` 5 and `priceMax` 12
- `5-12` sets `priceMin` 5 and `priceMax` 12
- `ages 5-12` sets `ageMin` 5 and `ageMax` 12
- `8 years old` sets `ageMin` 8 and `ageMax` 8
- `between 5 and 12 years old` sets `ageMin` 5 and `ageMax` 12
- `5-12 years old` sets `ageMin` 5 and `ageMax` 12
- Age 8 overlaps stored `6-12`
- Ages 5-12 overlap stored `6-12` and stored `12+`
- Ages 5-8 do not overlap stored `12+`
- A blank stored age does not overlap
- `red colored toy` returns no bounds
- A spaces-only phrase returns no bounds
- `Ninjago between 10 and 50 for ages 5-12` returns all three bounds
- The command exits 0
