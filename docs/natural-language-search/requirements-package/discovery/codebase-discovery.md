# Codebase discovery

Scoped to the confirmed change: augment search with AI-powered search so the customer experience is smooth. Written in business language. Confirmed by the user on 2026-09-23.

## What shoppers can do today

Shoppers can browse the catalog without signing in. They can open home, brand lists (LEGO, Qman, Keeppley), a category, new arrivals, discounts, and a product page.

## Search as it works today

Search already exists, and it is a name match. A shopper opens search from the header, types words, and sees products whose English name contains those words. They can narrow to one category or keep all categories. They can sort by price, lowest first (the default) or highest first. Results are shown 20 at a time, with further pages when there are more.

## What search does not do today

Search does not read the Vietnamese product name. It does not interpret a sentence, a theme, or an age group. An empty search still returns products. Signing in is not required.

## Nearby capabilities this change does not replace

Product details still lead to comments and the cart. Those stay as they are. The product list itself (English name, Vietnamese name, category, price, discount) is what search reads.
