# Dependency map

What the change needs from the shop. Confirmed by the user on 2026-09-23.

## From the shop as it is

- The product list, with an English price before discount, a theme name, and an age stored as text such as "12+" or "6–12."
- The search page the shopper already opens from the header, and the results list that page already shows, including "No products available" when nothing matches.
- Paging and opening a product, unchanged.

## Not in the shop today

- A language step that turns a phrase into those three facts. The shop has no such step. Which model supplies it is the solution phase. Until that step exists, the structured search cannot run.

## Not required

- Sign-in, the cart, comments, the Vietnamese price, the discounted price, color, or the product description.
